Asset ID:

`seven_roads_story2_cover_v1`

## Cover concept

Create a **vertical 2:3 storybook cover composition**.

Foreground:

- Temur, about 12, in his established blue/teal travelling clothes and King’s Rider belt;
- Samira, about 12, in her established red/burgundy travelling clothes and King’s Rider belt;
- both recognizable from the approved hero sheet;
- Shamol, Temur’s dark horse, and Bulut, Samira’s calm light grey/dapple horse, integrated naturally behind/beside them;
- the children should look alert and curious rather than triumphant or frightened.

Mid/background:

- the busy eastern caravan at Aras;
- covered wooden wagons, pack horses, merchants, textiles and practical road goods;
- pale-stone Aras walls/gate;
- restrained turquoise accents;
- **Aras camel banners only**;
- dry Central Asian mountain landscape.

The image should communicate **adventure + investigation + caravan mystery**, without depicting an exact spoiler scene.

## Spoiler protection

Do **not** show or reveal:

- the road seal;
- red wax impression;
- wax fragment;
- bandit token;
- broken strap fitting;
- Nadir’s hidden role;
- Rashid or Barlas framed as villains;
- guards secretly following Nadir;
- arrest or interrogation;
- bows/arrows from Choice 4;
- Sarvan’s two-tower clue;
- any text or captions.

If Rashid/Barlas or Nadir appear incidentally inside the distant caravan crowd, they must be unidentifiable background figures. Prefer not to feature them.

## Composition rules

- portrait / vertical, approximately **2:3**;
- visually compatible with the existing Story 1 cover;
- leave useful breathing room in the upper third for app title treatment/cropping;
- Temur and Samira must remain readable in mobile card crop;
- no text baked into the image;
- no decorative UI frame;
- no watermark;
- no collage panels;
- one coherent cover composition;
- no European medieval drift;
- no Disney/Pixar/glossy-3D look;
- no modern objects;
- no unnecessary magic.

## Style

Grounded Central Asian / Silk Road-inspired fantasy.

Painterly children’s adventure-book realism.

Warm natural morning/late-afternoon light, aged timber, natural fabrics, pale stone, dust, restrained turquoise/blue details, believable horses and wagons.

The mystery should come from composition and attention, not darkness or menace.

## Acceptance gate

Before approving the cover, verify:

1. Temur/Samira identities match the approved hero sheet.
2. Shamol/Bulut colors and tack remain canonical.
3. Aras uses **camel** symbol only.
4. Caravan matches Story 2 eastern-caravan reference.
5. No plot clue is revealed early.
6. No title/text is baked into the art.
7. Mobile 2:3 crop keeps both heroes readable.
8. Overall finish is consistent with the accepted Story 1 cover while remaining visually distinct.
