## Global visual prompt

Warm painterly children's adventure-book illustration in the exact approved QISSA Seven Roads visual style. Fictional Central Asian / Silk Road-inspired fantasy world, pre-industrial, expressive but natural children around age 12, rich environment, soft hand-painted texture, cinematic readable action, warm natural light, turquoise/gold/sand accents where appropriate. Not photorealistic, not glossy 3D animation, not comic-book rendering.

### Recurring identities

**Temur — `char_temur_v1`**
- Central Asian boy, around 12, lean athletic build;
- warm medium skin, dark brown/near-black straight hair, dark eyes;
- teal/blue riding chapan/tunic, beige trousers, brown boots;
- embroidered practical Central Asian-style riding cap;
- leather belt/satchel;
- short simple curved sabre in dark leather scabbard.
- After the Eastern Forest escape: right sleeve/forearm torn and dusty, and remains so through the ending.

**Samira — `char_samira_v1`**
- Central Asian girl, around 12, lean athletic build;
- dark eyes, long dark hair in one braid;
- burgundy/maroon riding chapan, beige trousers, riding boots;
- narrow decorative embroidered headband is allowed but must never look like a princess crown.
- Clean through Zaran; after the landslide/rescue, hem/knees/side are muddy and damp and remain so through the ending.

**Shamol — `horse_shamol_v1`**
- young dark bay, nearly black;
- black mane/tail;
- one small narrow white star on forehead;
- no white socks;
- teal tack/saddle blanket.

**Bulut — `horse_bulut_v1`**
- young light dapple-gray horse;
- darker gray mane/tail;
- no forehead star or blaze;
- burgundy tack/saddle blanket.

**King — `char_king_seven_roads_v1`**
- Central Asian man, late 40s;
- dark hair with some gray, neat graying beard;
- deep-blue robe/chapan;
- simple gold circlet with seven small raised points;
- dignified and warm, not an armored warlord.

**Royal guard captain — `char_royal_guard_captain_v1`**
- man in his 40s;
- dark-green guard coat;
- practical leather belt and sword;
- silver Seven Roads badge;
- disciplined civic/royal guard appearance.

**Ruler of Zaran — `char_zaran_ruler_v1`**
- man in his 50s;
- ochre/brown civic robe;
- local civic headwear, never a royal crown;
- calm administrative authority, visually belonging to Zaran rather than the capital court.

### World continuity

- capital: warm pale stone/plaster, arches, carved wood, restrained turquoise tile accents, broad courtyards, blue domes; no Gothic/European castle language.
- Zaran: warm golden-beige/honey stone, sunlit fields, gardens, flat/low-domed roof family, copper/bronze details, restrained turquoise accents; city should seem to hold warm light, not be literally plated in gold.
- Eastern Forest: dense mature old trees, exposed roots, narrow trail, layered green/brown shade.
- return-road landslide: mountain slope, blind bend, steep drop, road cut into terrain.

### Props

- road book: compact weathered brown leather cover, simple Seven Roads motif, cream pages, hand-drawn maps/road signs.
- royal outgoing letters: cream parchment, red wax seal, small dark-brown leather case.
- Zaran replies: cream parchment, distinct deep-blue wax seal, carried in the same practical leather cases.
- Temur's travel sabre: short, simple, curved; never oversized.
- bandit sign `symbol_bandits_v1`: rough carved crescent opening RIGHT, crossed by a short diagonal slash from lower-left to upper-right.

### Global exclusions

No modern technology, firearms, cars, electric lights, contemporary clothing, European Gothic castles, Arabian-palace cliché, direct copy of a historical dynasty, magic glow, text labels, UI text, captions, watermark, logos, gore, blood, weapon striking a person, adult armor on the children, sexualized/glamour styling, changing horse markings, changing costume colors, new unexplained royal symbols.

## Composition rules

- shared story art: landscape 4:3 preferred, crop-safe for 3:2 mobile display;
- cover: portrait-friendly composition with central safe area;
- choice pairs: same place, same light, same character state, similar camera distance; only the selected action should visibly differ;
- keep faces/actions away from extreme edges;
- never render Russian text into the image.

# Pair-generation rule for choices

Generate each A/B pair in one controlled pass or with the same seed/reference stack when possible:
- P3 jump vs precise control;
- P4 marker/book vs search upstream;
- P5 leave vs wait-until-visible;
- P6 verify vs second chance.

The pair should feel like two possible actions at the **same moment**, not two unrelated illustrations.

# Approval workflow

For each asset:
1. generate using the actual master + required reference images;
2. compare against World Bible and exact story state;
3. reject if face/horse markings/costume/damage/architecture drift;
4. owner reviews;
5. only after approval set asset status to `approved`;
6. upload approved final image to app-controlled storage;
7. resolve stable `asset_id` to runtime URL;
8. never overwrite an approved v1 asset with a redesign—create v2.
