## Character locks

### Danyar

- adult approximately 30–35;
- longer dark hair, uncovered;
- short beard/stubble;
- lean athletic road-officer build;
- important **BLUE scarf**;
- practical travelling clothes;
- broad King’s Rider belt;
- practical sabre remains sheathed;
- mounted on his established **grey working horse**.

He should read as experienced, alert and responsible — not a fantasy knight.

### Kamran

- young adult approximately 23–27;
- dark hair;
- clean or nearly clean-shaven;
- intelligent, controlled face;
- **rust/reddish scarf**;
- dark practical rider clothing;
- restrained grey travelling cloak;
- mounted on his established **dark horse**.

He may appear as the story's opposing force, but not as a theatrical villain.

## Royal Silver transport lock

The Royal Silver wagon must remain distinct from the prison wagon:

- heavy practical treasury transport;
- exactly four wheels / two axles;
- covered protected cargo body;
- strong timber lower body;
- restrained iron reinforcement;
- external driver bench;
- exactly **two working horses** harnessed to it;
- no barred windows;
- no prison door;
- no cage silhouette;
- no luxury royal-coach styling.

Do not expose the four iron chests as a giant treasure display. The cover is about guarded transport and road danger, not treasure spectacle.

## Cover concept

Create a vertical 2:3 storybook cover with **Danyar as the primary foreground identity**.

Foreground / lower-middle:

- Danyar mounted on his grey horse;
- blue scarf clearly readable;
- he is turning his attention toward the road ahead with restrained alertness;
- sabre remains sheathed;
- no combat pose.

Midground:

- the Royal Silver wagon travels on the road behind/beside him;
- exactly two harnessed working horses;
- external driver visible;
- a small number of mounted escort guards may be suggested without crowding;
- the wagon must remain readable in a mobile crop.

Secondary tension:

- Kamran on his dark horse appears farther along the road / on the opposing side of the composition;
- rust scarf and grey cloak identifiable but not oversized;
- his posture is controlled and watchful;
- he should feel connected to the coming danger without the cover literally showing the ambush mechanism.

Background:

- dry eastern road landscape of the Seven Roads world;
- low rocky slopes / open steppe-like terrain;
- restrained distant Ordan architecture may sit far behind if composition allows;
- warm natural light, dust, aged wood, leather and fabric.

The image should communicate:

**ROYAL TRANSPORT + EXPERIENCED RIDER + HIDDEN ROAD THREAT + PURSUIT/ADVENTURE**

without spelling out the whole attack.

## Spoiler protection

Do NOT show:

- the weighted rope thrown before the wagon horses;
- the rear pole obstruction;
- the exact attack formation;
- Danyar and Kamran duelling;
- Kamran captured;
- abandoned horse;
- prison wagon;
- hidden-network crescent/slash symbol;
- Story 2 seal mystery;
- Scar Man;
- Rustam;
- Temur or Samira;
- blood, wounds or injured horses;
- opened silver chests or piles of coins.

The cover must not reveal the mechanical solution or later capture.

## Composition rules

- portrait 2:3;
- leave visual breathing room in upper third;
- no title/text baked into the image;
- no decorative UI frame;
- no watermark;
- one coherent scene, no panels;
- Danyar must remain readable in center/mobile crop;
- wagon must remain identifiable but not dominate the entire page;
- Kamran must be visible enough to add tension, but secondary to Danyar;
- keep all important faces away from extreme crop edges.

## Style

Warm painterly children’s adventure-book realism.

Central Asian / Silk Road-inspired historical fantasy.

Believable horses, road equipment and wagon construction.

Warm natural light with restrained blue/rust accents coming from the two character identities.

Serious adventure, not grimdark.

No photorealism.
No glossy 3D.
No Disney/Pixar look.
No European medieval-knight aesthetic.
No magic glow.
No modern objects.
