## Cover — Festival of Courage

**Purpose:** communicate adventure, friendship/rivalry, horses and the Seven Roads world without spoiling the bandits or final bahadur ceremony.

**Scene:** Temur on Shamol and Samira on Bulut at the edge of the capital square, palace and seven festival banners behind them, seven roads implied beyond the city.

**State:** pre-departure; both outfits clean.

**Must preserve:**
- Temur = teal/blue family, embroidered riding cap;
- Samira = burgundy/red family, long braid, narrow decorative headband (not crown);
- Shamol = dark bay/almost black + small white forehead star;
- Bulut = light dapple gray, no white blaze;
- Central Asian / Silk Road fantasy architecture.

---

## Illustration 1 — The Festival square

**Story moment:** opening / sixteen participants gather.

**Composition:** wide establishing shot of the capital in its mountain valley; bright main square; palace steps; seven long banners; children and families gathered.

**Narrative job:** establish the kingdom's scale and visual language before focusing on the two heroes.

**Do not show yet:**
- bahadur ceremonial belts or sabres;
- bandit sign;
- damaged clothing.

---

## Illustration 2 — Temur and Samira are selected

**Story moment:** after running, archery and horse-preparation trials, only two remain.

**Composition:** palace steps and crowd; Temur and Samira standing separately, exchanging the first competitive look; king above/between them.

**State:** strangers / new rivals, not friendly pose.

**Important:** visual body language should not imply they are already best friends.

---

## Illustration 3 — Shamol, Bulut and the road book

**Story moment:** royal stable.

**Composition:** Temur reaching toward Shamol; Samira beside Bulut, who is trying to reach the apple; small road book visible in Samira's hands or satchel.

**Narrative job:** introduce both horses as characters and plant the road book.

**Continuity anchors:**
- Shamol's forehead star;
- Bulut's dapple gray coat;
- horse tack colors;
- road book brown leather cover.

---

## Illustration 4 — Departure from the capital

**Story moment:** trumpet sounds and the two riders leave.

**Composition:** energetic forward movement through/away from the main gate; festival crowd and warm pale-stone/turquoise architecture behind.

**Reference:** `02_capital_festival_approved.png`

**State:** both clean; letter cases and road books carried.

---

## Illustration 5 — Fallen tree and the first lie

**Story moment:** blocked road; two alternative paths.

**Composition:** fallen tree across main road. Upper path visible climbing between hills; forest path visible in another direction. Samira with road book; Temur waiting for her answer.

**Critical emotional beat:** Samira hesitates before saying «Через лес. Эта закрыта.»

**Do not make her look villainous.** She is competitive and knowingly makes a bad choice; expression should show hesitation/conflict.

---

## Illustration 6 — The bandit sign

**Story moment:** Temur enters the Eastern Forest and notices the carved mark before the ambush.

**Composition:** Temur and Shamol on narrow forest trail; large old tree near foreground bearing `symbol_bandits_v1`.

**Sign geometry is strict:**
- crescent opens right;
- short slash lower-left → upper-right.

**Narrative job:** plant a visual clue before danger appears.

---

## Illustration 7 — Escape from the bandits / lost road book

**Story moment:** Temur cuts the rope and Shamol bursts through; during the chase Temur's road book falls from his satchel.

**Composition:** dynamic chase. Temur uses sabre **on the rope**, not on a person. Bandits remain behind/at distance. If the frame includes the book loss, show the small brown road book caught between the exposed roots of a **large forked oak** immediately beyond the cut rope.

**Reference:** `03_eastern_forest_escape_approved.png`

**Strict geography:** the lost book is:
- in the Eastern Forest;
- on the narrow escape trail;
- just after the cut rope;
- between roots of a large forked oak;
- before the later ditch jump.

**State transition:** Temur's right sleeve/forearm becomes torn and dusty during/after this sequence; from this point forward he no longer carries his road book.

**Future-world hook:** the lost book remains physically in the forest after Story 1 unless a later story explicitly moves/retrieves it.

**Safety:** no wounds, blood or weapon strike on a human.

---

## Illustration 8 — Samira chooses the safe crossing

**Story moment:** flooded ford after rain; Bulut stops; Samira checks the road book.

**Composition:** muddy fast water in foreground, stone road marker, Samira reading the small road book beside/atop Bulut; old stone crossing visible upstream.

**Narrative job:** show that reading and attention matter materially.

**State:** Samira still clean; Bulut consistent.

---

## Illustration 9 — First sight of Zaran

**Story moment:** Samira reaches the city first.

**Composition:** Samira and Bulut on approach, golden-warm Zaran ahead among fields.

**Reference:** `04_zaran_arrival_approved.png`

**Zaran identity:**
- warm golden-beige walls;
- sunlit honey-colored stone;
- gardens;
- flat/low-domed roof family;
- carved wood / copper-bronze details;
- restrained turquoise accents;
- fields with gold grasses / wheat around greener cultivated land.

**Avoid:** European castle silhouette, literal gold-plated city.

---

## Illustration 10 — Temur reports the bandits in Zaran

**Story moment:** ruler and local guard examine Temur's drawn mark.

**Composition:** civic interior, warm daylight; Temur visibly dusty with torn right sleeve; ruler and city guard looking at the drawn crescent-slash sign.

**Narrative job:** show that Temur's observation has consequences beyond his own escape.

**Props:** intact letter/reply protocol; drawing must match `symbol_bandits_v1`.

---

## Illustration 11 — Samira sees the landslide

**Story moment:** return road; she has a clear chance to take the detour and preserve her lead but understands Temur may not see the danger.

**Composition:** mountain road along slope, blind bend, broken road ahead; Samira looking back toward Temur's approach and toward the detour.

**Narrative job:** moral decision before physical accident.

**Do not yet show Samira fallen.**

---

## Illustration 12 — Landslide rescue

**Story moment:** Samira on ledge; Bulut above; Temur has stopped safely and help arrives.

**Reference:** `05_landslide_rescue_approved.png`

**State:**
- Temur = torn/dusty right sleeve from forest;
- Samira = mud/damp-earth marks after fall;
- Bulut = above road, safe;
- adults + ropes perform actual rescue.

**Important:** Temur is brave because he gets competent adult help, not because he attempts a reckless solo climb.

---

## Illustration 13 — The confession and damaged trust

**Story moment:** after rescue, Samira admits the first lie; shortly afterward she suggests another route and Temur asks to verify the road book himself.

**Composition option A:** quieter road-post / roadside confession; horses nearby; children facing each other with emotional distance but no melodrama.

**Composition option B:** Samira holds the open road book toward Temur; Temur reads the route himself before agreeing to follow it.

**Key emotional state:**
- Samira = remorseful but direct;
- Temur = hurt/angry but not cruel;
- trust is visibly reduced but not destroyed;
- not reconciled into instant cheerful friendship yet.

**Useful dialogue beats:** «Я всё равно рад, что ты жива. Но сердиться ещё буду.» / «Покажи.»

---

## Illustration 14 — Return together

**Story moment:** capital visible ahead; Shamol and Bulut move side by side.

**Composition:** sunset / late-afternoon approach to capital.

**State:** all journey damage remains visible.

**Narrative job:** show changed relationship without needing a hug or explicit friendship pose.

---

## Illustration 15 — The king reads both replies

**Story moment:** palace square; both sealed replies delivered; king reads Samira's first, then Temur's longer report.

**Composition:** king, captain, two children, horses nearby if framing allows.

**Continuity:**
- Samira's reply confirms first arrival in Zaran;
- Temur's reply contains bandit warning;
- both reply seals intact until the king opens them.

---

## Illustration 16 — Two young bahadurs of the realm

**Story moment:** the standing bahadur ceremony has just been completed.

**Composition:** Temur and Samira stand upright side by side in the capital square. Their original travel-worn clothes remain visible beneath broad ceremonial belts with metal buckles; ceremonial sabres stay in scabbards at the belts. The ruler and captain/senior warrior remain part of the ceremonial context, with crowd, realm banners, Shamol and Bulut behind if framing allows.

**Important:** no kneeling, no blade-to-shoulder gesture, no European knighting pose, and no adult war-armor transformation. They are still the same children in the same journey outfits.

**Humor option in secondary action:** Bulut noticing food near the festive table.

---

## Illustration 17 — Final hook

**Story moment:** after celebration; Temur catches sight of the king and captain studying the bandit mark.

**Composition:** foreground/side Temur noticing; in background king + captain + paper bearing exact sign; road/forest direction hinted beyond city.

**Narrative job:** close Story 1 while opening the wider-world thread.

**Do not reveal:** bandit leader, hideout, motive or solution.

# Visual continuity checklist before each generated image

Before image generation verify:

1. Which story state (S0–S7) is active?
2. Is Temur's sleeve intact or torn at this point?
3. Is Samira clean or muddy at this point?
4. Does Shamol still have the same small white forehead star?
5. Is Bulut still light dapple gray with no white blaze?
6. Are outfit/tack color families unchanged?
7. Are letter case / road book / sabre designs unchanged, and is Temur's road book absent after the forest loss?
8. Is the bandit mark geometry exact?
9. Does the location match its approved architecture/geography?
10. Does the child age remain around 12?
11. Is the approved painterly master style being used?
12. Are incidental generated-image details being prevented from becoming accidental new canon?
