- **Temur:** ~12, lean Central Asian boy, short dark hair; main riding silhouette a deep teal knee-length chapan/tunic, beige trousers, brown riding boots, belt, small satchel, manageable sheathed curved sabre on official duties. The **approved hero/horse sheet depicts a small blue patterned cap**; this is a visual-reference identifier, but is not separately mandated by the prose of World Bible V2. The owner-selected master scene already contains **cream garments with teal outer cloak**; existence of the visual does not itself define where wardrobe transitions happen.

- Ceremonial Rider's belts are received at Festival's end and clearly reappear in S2E2 Part 5/Part 10 and E4. They are not inseparable body parts: may be removed at home or during sleep; during official service keep them according to scenes.

## 5. Suggested editorial handling — no silent prose change

1. **Do not** insert a laundry or sewing passage into every episode. Ordinary home life between significant scenes is sufficient causal room. Routine care is a visual implication, not always necessary in the written narrative.
2. Use the existing **E3 Sarvan-home wash invitation** as the clearest explicit washing anchor. Do not convert it into an unearned claim that clothes were fully laundered/dried in minutes.
3. If the owner wants one minimal prose bridge for the E5 costume contrast, the **only candidate** is S2E5 Part 1 `post_choice_text`, during the stop at Rustam's road post and **before** «Они выехали из поста.» (UZ: «Ular postdan chiqib ketishdi.»). The owner must approve the exact wording/translation first. **Do not add it now**; check whether simple visual layer continuity suffices.
4. Make exact costume states per **scene time**, not per episode: two consecutive shots from the same ride use the same garment and accumulated wear; after a documented home/night rest a new state becomes *possible*, not compulsory.
5. Route outcomes in E5 are mutually exclusive; plateau wind/forest proximity may affect dust or cape movement, **not** deterministic clothing identities or hidden safety answers. On rejoining the post, use the same compatible neutral garment state.

## 6. Future image-generation and technical acceptance gates

For each new illustration, fill a short editorial record **before generation**:
`story_number`, `part_id`, `phase`, exact RU/UZ paragraph anchor, narrative time (`morning/evening/overnight/continuous`), character presence, `base_outfit`, `removable_layers`, `wear_state`, `last_confirmed_home_or_rest`, horse/tack IDs, intended narrative information visible to a child, and links to the approved reference images.

Reject images for:
- a sudden pristine costume in the same unbroken ride/fight;
- hard switched Temur/Samira color identities without established layer/alternate;
- incorrect age, faces, braid, Shamol/Bulut coat or saddle blankets;
- costume change at a time when a character is absent or never returned home;
- modern fabrics, insignia, text/signs, unmentioned scars/blood, story spoilers;
- layering inconsistent with movement, saddles and reins.

**Application architecture:** record costume continuity in an *editorial sidecar* first. Do **not** add live database fields, alter release flags, generate images automatically or fork localized art based on costume alone. One approved image remains shared across RU/UZ, as today. Version a future visual-canon delta separately if the owner approves a second complete outfit or formally codifies the image-only headwear.
