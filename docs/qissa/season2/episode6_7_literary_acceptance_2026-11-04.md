## Corrections completed in this pass

1. Restored the intentional **Kamran / Farid** distinction in the gang-leader ending, including one clarifying sentence for listeners.
2. Explicitly accounted for the other two wagon arrests in Episode 6 choice B, avoiding an unexplained total.
3. Clarified eight tower-side attackers: one engages Rahim, two seek the flank, five pressure Rustam's group.
4. Removed an awkward Temur line and simplified the spoken counting of reinforcements.
5. Clarified escort of seven captured bandits while six convoy guards stay with the wagon.
6. Removed a repeated Daniyar aphorism at the start of Episode 7 (its message already closed Episode 6).
7. Split the long wax-imprint explanation into shorter audible ideas and simplified the subsequent inference.
8. Moved the central **theft was intended to copy the seal, not steal silver** deduction to the child protagonists; both investigation paths lead to the same documented evidence without pretending they found the same first clue.

## Read-aloud assessment

- **Narrative voices:** speaker tags and short exchanges generally separate Temur, Samira, Daniyar, Rustam, Rahim and Said.
- **Causal clarity:** the threat, chosen action, results on the other front, capture totals and return decision are explicit.
- **High information load:** the seal-copy explanation in Episode 7 is shorter but is still a technical puzzle; a child-listening check should ask “Почему копия печати получилась не совсем такой же?”
- **Excitement and emotional recovery:** Episode 6 retains active sword-fighting, a visible decision payoff, and post-battle tension release without graphic description.
- **Boundaries:** editorial inference is **not** an actual child listening test, TTS audio QA, or independent assessment by a native Uzbek literary editor.

## Verdict and scope of responsibility

**Editorial staging accepted conditionally.** The identified textual and continuity defects were repaired in both languages. Automated CI can certify schema and regression behavior, not artistic merit or actual listener comprehension. Publication state is unchanged.
